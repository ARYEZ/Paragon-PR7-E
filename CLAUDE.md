# Paragon PR7-E: notes for Claude

This repository starts as a copy of **Paragon PR7-B** (the bass processor, repo `ARYEZ/Paragon-PR7-B`) at PR7-B **0.11.0**, full history included, to become **Paragon PR7-E**, the **utility** processor of the PR7 family. Its siblings: **PR7-G** (guitar, repo `ARYEZ/Paragon-PR7-G`), **PR7-B** (bass), **PR7-V** (vocal, `ARYEZ/Paragon-PR7-V`) and **PR7-C** (`ARYEZ/Paragon-PR7-C`). Everything below describes how the project is built, tested and released, and how the owner likes to work.

## First: make it its own plugin (done in 0.1.0)

All of this was done in 0.1.0; it's kept as the record of what makes PR7-E its own plugin. (The loader thread named in item 5 went with the NAM amp, so there's no thread to rename.)

   - `BUNDLE_ID` becomes `com.paragon.paragonpr7e`.

   - Keep `PLUGIN_MANUFACTURER_CODE Prgn` (same maker).
   - Restart the version at `0.1.0` (`project(ParagonX1 VERSION ...)`; the CMake target name can stay).
2. **Rigs folder** (`source/rigs/RigLibrary.cpp`): "Paragon PR7-E". Remove any carry-over from older folders, and update the rename test in `tests/RigTests.cpp` to match.
3. **Settings file** (`source/ui/Finish.cpp`): `applicationName` becomes "Paragon PR7-E", so PR7-E remembers its own finish.
4. **Logo** (`source/ui/Logo.cpp`): the mark's "-B" becomes "-E", drawn in the same slanted, speed-cut lettering.
5. **Text:** the About text and MIDI hint in `source/ui/DockPanels.cpp`, the loader thread name in `PluginProcessor.h`, and `README.md` all say PR7-E.
6. **Release workflow** (`.github/workflows/build.yml`, `.github/release-install.md`):
   - Bundle paths become `Paragon PR7-E.vst3`.
   - Zips become `ParagonPR7-E-${TAG}-...`.
   - The release title becomes "Paragon PR7-E ${TAG}".
7. **CHANGELOG.md:** start fresh at 0.1.0 ("The utility processor, based on Paragon PR7-B 0.11.0").
8. **Re-enable releases.** The `release` job's `if:` was set to `false && ...` in the seed commit so this repo didn't publish a bass release. Put it back to `github.event_name == 'push'` in the same commit as the version bump.

## The plan: seven stomps, each modelled on a specific plugin

The owner's layout (0.12.0) follows a **DigiTech GSP21 Legend foot controller** (the owner sent a photo): the display centred along the top, **the seven stomps in one row**, and under them **five rigs, BANK down and BANK up** (the GSP's five-preset mode: `rigPages` in `MainView.cpp`, five rigs to a page within each bank; BANK changes the page without loading, and the row follows the loaded rig). No expression pedal (removed in 0.12.0 at the owner's ask: it could only be a volume pedal; its parameters stay for old projects but do nothing). Small keys: UNDO, REDO, BYPASS (TUNER, RESET and TAP went, and the header's BPM / TAP). Before that (0.1.0 to 0.11.0) it was PR7-V's three over four with a pedal. Done in 0.1.0: the face came from PR7-V, and the bass-specific blocks (NAM amp, cab IR, Obsidian, Gotham, the bass rigs) are gone. Factory rigs (0.6.0, `source/rigs/FactoryRigs.cpp`, asked for by the owner): starting points by bank (EQ; COMP on four, DOMINION on four, the LIMITER on the mastering ones), A vocals and voice (Init at A01), B drums, C bass/guitars/keys, D full mix, E mastering. Common mixing moves, our own; dynamic bands for intermittent problems; at most +4.5 dB anywhere and ±2 dB for mastering (tested). Add to them as stomps are built (e.g. the limiter in the mastering rigs). **Genre rigs (0.15.0, the owner's ask):** `addGenre` makes six per genre (Vocal A, Drums B, Guitars C, Bass C2x, Mix D, Master E) at x11 to x19 in the order modern metal, metalcore, djent, nu metal, argent metal (the owner's term for the dense industrial-djent sound of the 2016-2020 DOOM game soundtracks; never name the game or composer on the plugin), hard rock, grunge, symphonic metal, industrial. Their COMP and DOMINION settings are copied from the genre presets of the same name (`makeGenres` in `CompPresets.cpp` and `DominionPresets.cpp`, category `Genres/<genre>`: a submenu under GENRES in the PRESET menu); tests check they match. The rig row starts a page at each ten (`rigPages`). Our own interpretations, not tuned by ear; the owner researched producer presets first, but none are published as mixing presets, and real people's names stay off the plugin. In 0.3.0 the owner had PR7-B's general effects (pitch, comp / gate, filter / pitch, glitch, modulation with AEON, delay, reverb, lo-fi) removed: they were mono and lost the right channel. PR7-E is **stereo throughout**: every new stomp must process both channels (or one copy per channel) and keep left and right apart. The `Circuit` solver stays (tested) and may be useful later.

Stomps not built yet are placeholders: a `Footswitch` with no parameter IDs (dark LED, the pad does nothing, the name opens nothing; see `addStomp` in `MainView.cpp`). Give a stomp its parameters only when it's built: once released, IDs are saved forever. Stomp 4 (EQ) switches the graphic EQ until the planned EQ replaces it. Planning order: one stomp at a time, with the owner.

- **Top row**
  1. **ORACLE** (the owner's name; was the TUNER), **melody to MIDI, inspired by Melodyne and VariAudio: done in 0.11.0.** The tuner (0.2.0, `dsp/Tuner.*`: checking the pitch of recorded parts, single parts up to 1,500 Hz, never changes the sound) is part of it: the unit's screen, and the note now on ORACLE's page. ORACLE (`dsp/oracle/Oracle.*`, our own design): while `tunerOn` and the DAW plays, the processor's timer reads every 10 ms of the input tap (YIN pitch and level, placed in the song from the host position, which the audio thread passes as `TimelineMark`s); a pass over a passage replaces what was heard there. `findNotes`: notes end at silences, at a move to another note (pitch smoothed over ~150 ms so vibrato doesn't split, judged against the median of the last 300 ms) or a fresh attack; pitch is the middle of the note. Owner's choices: drag a MIDI file onto a track (Reaper, Studio One, Cubase; not live MIDI out), fix the notes before export (`ui/OraclePage.*`: piano roll, own undo), the name. Not parameters: the notes, SENSITIVITY, MIN NOTE and QUANTIZE are saved in the plugin state (an `Oracle` child, taken out before `replaceState`). Tests: `tests/OracleTests.cpp`. Not built: pitch correction of the audio (offered for later), chords, ARA (the DAW handing over the audio file), live MIDI out, AUv3 for BeatMaker.
  2. **COMP: done in 0.9.0**, the owner's choice for the open slot: PR7-C 0.4.1's compressor ported unchanged (`dsp/effects/Compressor.*`; its tests in `tests/CompressorTests.cpp`, minus the SC-filter one: PR7-E has no sidechain filters, so the detector hears the audio itself). Parameters `comp{On,Threshold,Ratio,Attack,Release,Auto,MakeUp,Character,Link}` (version hint 33; characters VCA, OPTO, FET by index; RATIO uses PR7-C's marked range). After the EQ, before OUTPUT and the LIMITER. **Bands: done in 0.13.0**, inspired by FabFilter Pro-MB (the owner supplied its manual: don't commit it); our own design, since Pro-MB's "Dynamic Phase" isn't published (`dsp/effects/MultibandComp.*`): up to six freely placed bands, each a pair of the EQ's matched high-order shelves on the whole signal (no crossover split; a 0 dB shelf is exactly no filter, so an idle band changes nothing), a band-pass detector with COMP's VCA/OPTO/FET detectors (FET's grit stays the full band's), MODE x RANGE sign for the four kinds, RATIO, KNEE, STEREO (both with LINK, mid, side), OUTPUT, per-band LOOK-AHEAD under a global switch (COMP 20 ms late, reported to the DAW from the timer), MIX 0-200%, SOLO. Owner's choices: in COMP (not a new stomp), zero latency plus look-ahead, all band features, COMP's characters per band, presets. **Presets: done in 0.14.0** (`rigs/CompPresets.*`: every `comp*` parameter but `compOn`, and every `mb*` one; `.pr7comp` files in the rig folder's "Comp Presets"), on the shared `rigs/StompPresets.h` (a template on traits: file extension, XML tag, `holds`, factory list) and `ui/PresetButton.*`, which DOMINION's presets use too. Parameters (version hint 35): `compWide` (the full band, after the bands; on by default so older rigs are unchanged), `mbLookahead`, `mbMix`, `mbB{n}{Used,On,Low,High,Slope,Thresh,Range,Mode,Ratio,Knee,Attack,Release,Char,Stereo,Link,Out,Look}` for n = 1 to 6 (never reorder `multiband::modeNames`, `stereoNames`). Page: `ui/CompPage.*` (stomp 2's name and the chain's COMP open it). Tests: `tests/MultibandTests.cpp`.
  3. **Insert launcher.** A plugin can't open the other plugins on the DAW's track: hosts don't allow it. What it *can* do is host plugins itself (JUCE `AudioPluginFormatManager` / `KnownPluginList`, VST3 on Windows): load plugins into slots inside PR7-E and open their editors from the stomp, a mini rack. Confirm with the owner which platform it's for: the owner works in BeatMaker, and on iPad plugins are AUv3, which works differently.
- **Bottom row**
  1. **EQ inspired by FabFilter Pro-Q 4.** The owner supplied its manual (don't commit it: it's FabFilter's). Agreed: it **replaces the graphic EQ**, opens on a **big EQ page** of its own (over the pedal and dock, like PRESETS), and is built in stages: (1) the core EQ: **done in 0.4.0** (`dsp/eq/EqDesign.*`: shapes, slopes and the matched filters; `dsp/eq/ParametricEq.*`: the processing; `ui/EqDisplay.*`, `ui/EqPage.*`; tests in `tests/EqTests.cpp`). Band parameters are `eqB{n}{Used,On,Shape,Freq,Gain,Q,Slope,Place}` (version hint 30) and `eqB{n}{Dyn,DynAuto,Thresh,Attack,Release}` (31) for n = 1 to 24: never reorder `eq::shapeNames`, `slopeNames` or `placementNames`. (2) **dynamic EQ: done in 0.5.0** (`dsp/eq/Dynamics.h`: the gain curve, knee, AUTO threshold and timing, all our own numbers; the detector is in `ParametricEq`). Owner's choices: auto by default with manual threshold/attack/release; cut and boost (negative and positive range); no sidechain for now (BeatMaker on iPad may not route one). (3) **EQ Match: done in 0.7.0** (`dsp/eq/EqMatch.*`: long-term spectra, the target and the band fitting; `dsp/eq/EqMatchSession.*`: listening from the processor's timer, file loading, saved references in the rigs folder's "Match References" as `.pr7ref`; `ui/EqMatchPanel.*`). Our own analysis and fitting; references from the input, a file or saved, no sidechain. Adds ordinary bands (no new parameters). Not wanted: linear phase, character modes. Skipped: instance list, surround, MIDI learn, spectral dynamics (that's DOMINION's job). "Natural Phase" is FabFilter's own, unpublished.
  2. **DOMINION** (the owner's name), **inspired by oeksound soothe 3: done in 0.10.0.** The owner supplied soothe 3's manual (oeksound's: don't commit it). Our own design (`dsp/dominion/Dominion.*`): a bank of 1/6-octave detectors 1/12 octave apart, 20 Hz to 20 kHz; SOFT compares each part with its neighbourhood (leaving out the nearest two either side), HARD with a fixed level set by DEPTH (-20 dB less 3 dB per step, tilted 1.5 dB/oct); the cut is a bell per filter whose gains are solved (Gauss-Seidel) so the overlapping bells make the cut wanted; attack/release frequency-dependent with tilts. Owner's choices: zero latency only (no HQ/linear-phase modes); a big page (`ui/DominionPage.*`); depth-curve bands, stereo/M-S with link and focus, tilts, delta and band listen. Parameters `dom*` and `domB{n}{Used,On,Shape,Freq,Depth,Q,Slope,Focus}` for n = 1 to 8 (version hint 34; never reorder `dominion::modeNames`, `stereoNames`, `shapeNames`). After the EQ, before COMP. Its own presets (0.10.3, the owner's ask, apart from the rigs): `rigs/DominionPresets.*`, factory ones by category plus the user's `.pr7dom` files in the rig folder's "Dominion Presets"; a preset holds every `dom*` parameter except `domOn`. Tests: `tests/DominionTests.cpp`. Not built: sidechain, multichannel, the delayed quality modes.
  3. **Inspired by Soundtheory Gullfoss** ("maybe", per the owner). Also a secret, adaptive algorithm: our own design. Ask for the manual.
  4. **The output trim / limiter from PR7-C** (`ARYEZ/Paragon-PR7-C`): **done in 0.8.0**, ported from PR7-C 0.4.1 (`dsp/effects/Limiter.*`, unchanged but for `process()` taking a block; PR7-C's limiter tests ported in `tests/LimiterTests.cpp`). Parameters `limitOn/Threshold/MakeUp/Mode/Mix/Link/Trim` (version hint 32; modes Brick, Soft, Auto by index). Last in the chain, after OUTPUT and RIG LEVEL. **Upgraded in 0.16.0, inspired by FabFilter Pro-L 2** (the owner supplied its manual: don't commit it); our own design (`dsp/effects/MasterLimiter.*`, `dsp/effects/Loudness.*`): GAIN into an OUTPUT ceiling; a look-ahead transient stage (moving minimum, then moving average over the look-ahead: provably never over the ceiling) and a slower release stage (ATTACK, RELEASE), each with its own LINK; eight styles appended to `limitModes` (Clear, Punchy, Dynamic, All-round, Aggressive, Modern, Glue, Safe: `limiter::characterOf`); Brick/Soft/Auto stay the old `effects::Limiter` (no look-ahead etc.). TRUE PEAK (an 8x, 64-tap detector, then a 1 ms last stage 0.1 dB under; within 0.1 dB of a 150-tap reference on band-limited material, 0.2 dB for Aggressive with no look-ahead or oversampling), 2x-8x OVERSAMPLING (JUCE's FIR), UNITY and DELTA (not in rigs), DC FILTER, TRIM and TPDF DITHER with three shapings inside it, last. Loudness after BS.1770-4 / R128 (tested against EBU Tech 3341/3342). Its delay goes to the DAW (`updateLatency`); switching on waits for the look-ahead to fill before fading in. Owner's choices: upgrade stomp 7, look-ahead with a zero option, all extras, plain descriptive style names. Parameters (version hint 36): `limit{Gain,Ceiling,Lookahead,Attack,Release,LinkTransient,LinkRelease,TruePeak,Oversampling,Dither,Shaping,Unity,Delta,DcFilter}`; older projects and rigs convert THRESHOLD/MAKE UP to GAIN/OUTPUT (`convertOldLimiter`, the same sound). Page: `ui/LimiterPage.*` (stomp 7's name and the chain's LIMITER open it; the loudness target and time scale are properties on the state). The mastering factory rigs use MODERN with TRUE PEAK at -1 dBTP. Tests: `tests/MasterLimiterTests.cpp`. Not built: presets, side chain, surround, the display modes, K-meter scales.
- **Names:** none of those products' names or branding on the plugin: our own names (the owner names them, as with Obsidian, Gotham, AEON). "Inspired by" in descriptions is fine.

## The project

- An AGPLv3 **JUCE VST3** plugin, drawn as a floor processor laid out like a GSP21 foot controller, sharing the PR7 family's code.
- **Code layout:** DSP in `source/dsp/effects/`, UI in `source/ui/`, parameters in `source/Parameters.*`, rigs in `source/rigs/`, tests in `tests/`.
- **Parameters:**
  - Saved by ID and choices by **index**: only ever *append* choices, never reorder.
  - New parameters get a new version hint number.
  - Migrations for changed defaults live in `setStateInformation` and `Rig::fromXml`.
- **Shared DSP pieces:** `BypassFade` and `TailWatch` (EffectUtils.h), and `Circuit` (Circuit.h: models analog stages from their schematics, part by part).

## Build, test, check

- **Build:** `cmake -B build && cmake --build build -j8`.
- **Tests (Catch2):** `xvfb-run -a build/tests/ParagonX1Tests`. All must pass. New features get tests that check what the feature really does to the sound (levels, frequencies, harmonics), not just that it runs.
- **pluginval:** `xvfb-run -a <pluginval> --strictness-level 10 --validate-in-process "build/ParagonX1_artefacts/Release/VST3/<name>.vst3"`. Check its exit code. CI runs it too.
- **Screenshots** for checking the UI, via `xvfb-run -a build/tests/ParagonX1Tests "[snapshot]"` with environment variables:
  - `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT` (output path), `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_WIDTH` (e.g. 2560 for 2x).
  - `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_BLOCK=n` (open block n's panel), `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_PARAMS=id=value,...`.
  - `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_PAGE=presets|settings|eq`, `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_FINISH=black|neon`.
  - `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_BLOCK=0` (the dock's EQ overview), `1` (DOMINION's page), `2` (COMP's page), or `3` (the LIMITER); `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_TUNER=1` (the tuner on, after a sung phrase: the unit's screen shows the note).
  - `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_PAGE=comp` (COMP's page) with `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_COMP=1` (four bands working on pink noise with a loud 60 Hz and 3 kHz, band 3 selected); `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_COMP_PRESET=name` loads one of its factory presets instead.
  - `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_PAGE=limiter` (the LIMITER's page) with `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_LIMITER=1` (MODERN, TRUE PEAK, a drum loop driven 8 dB into it); `"[snapshot-preset-menu]"` draws COMP's GENRES submenus (`PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_GENRE=name`).
  - `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_PAGE=oracle` (ORACLE's page) with `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_ORACLE=1` (a sung line heard at 100 BPM from bar 2, one note selected).
  - `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_PAGE=dominion` (DOMINION's page) with `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_DOMINION=1` (on, three depth-curve bands, pink noise with resonances at 180 Hz, 2.5 kHz and 6.3 kHz through it); `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_DOMINION_PRESET=name` loads one of its factory presets instead of the three bands.
  - `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_EQ=1` (a mix-bus set of EQ bands with noise through the analyser; band 3 is dynamic), `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_EQ_BAND=n` (select band n), `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_MATCH=1` (with `PAGE=eq`: EQ Match open, having heard a track and a brighter reference).
  - Menus open in their own window, so the editor snapshot can't show one: `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT=menu.png ... "[snapshot-menu]"` draws a drop-down as the plugin's look-and-feel does (black, white text, the red-pink-orange highlight, as the owner asked in 0.10.1).
  - Render, look, crop and zoom before claiming a visual change is right.

## Releases

- Bump `project(ParagonX1 VERSION x.y.z)` in CMakeLists.txt and add a matching `## x.y.z` section at the top of CHANGELOG.md, then push.
- CI builds Windows, macOS and Linux and publishes a GitHub Release with the three zips, using that changelog section as the notes. It publishes from a push to **any** branch whose version isn't released yet.
- After pushing, check the release published (all three zips) about 15 minutes later, **and that its `target_commitish` is your commit**. If the version was already taken, CI skips silently and the release you'd link is someone else's build.
- **One chat per plugin at a time.** In October 2026 two chats released PR7-G and PR7-V under the same version numbers: one chat's releases were skipped, and features (and a UI fix) went missing from the builds the owner installed. Before choosing a version, check the repo's tags *and* other branches for unreleased work; if another branch is ahead, merge it first so every release contains everything.
- The owner installs from GitHub Releases on **Windows**.

## How the owner likes to work

- **Explain the why**, specifically: what was wrong, what changed, and the trade-offs. Plain language; the owner is a musician, not a DSP engineer.
- **Be honest about limits:**
  - Say when something is our design rather than a copy ("modelled on", "inspired by").
  - Say when a detail is a guess.
  - Say what can't be checked here (audio by ear).
  - Ask for source material, such as manuals, program charts or gear rundowns, rather than invent specifics.
- **No trademarked names or branding** on our models ("Daybreak", "GLITCH", "Obsidian", "Gotham", "AEON"). "Inspired by" in descriptions is fine.
- **Show it:** send a rendered screenshot of any visual change.
- **"merge it"** means: open a PR from the working branch to main and merge it, then reset the working branch to the new main.
- Commit messages explain what and why.
